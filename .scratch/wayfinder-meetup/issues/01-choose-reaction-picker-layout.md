## Question

Which journey through wayfinder should the audience see during the 17-minute reaction-picker demo? Decide which of planning, research, prototyping, and execution should happen live, which can be illustrated with prepared states, and what visible outcome demonstrates the value of organizing work. Explain the flexible stopping points without implying that every project needs every mode or that a single wayfinder invocation performs the whole journey.

The prepared scaffold and prebuilt POC are agreed preparation choices. Whether to chart live, which human decision to show, and what implementation step to attempt remain open. This ticket chooses the teaching journey, not the app's detailed feature list.

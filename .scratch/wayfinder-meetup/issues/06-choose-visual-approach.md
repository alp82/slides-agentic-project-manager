# Choose a visual approach for the slides and reaction picker

Type: prototype
Label: wayfinder:prototype
Status: open
Assignee: unassigned
Parent: [Make the wayfinder meetup presentation](../map.md)
Blocked by: 03, 04

## Question

What visual approach makes the short slide story and scoped reaction picker legible, fun, and coherent on a projector? Compare rough slide and app treatments with the presenter, including how much space the picker, preview, and technical explanations need. Resolve the approach from actual human feedback, not from an agent-selected default.

## Assets

The [premature POC](../../../demo/reaction-picker-prototype/README.md) contains Studio, Spotlight, and Compact variants. They are optional starting points; the choice is not restricted to them. No variant has been approved.

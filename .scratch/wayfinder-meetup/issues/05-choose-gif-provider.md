## Question

Which GIF provider should the scoped POC use, and what should the audience see if the service or network is unavailable? Choose between GIPHY and KLIPY based on available access, the intended searches, and setup effort. Identify any concrete access or research prerequisite needed before choosing; do not infer a choice from the premature POC's default configuration.

Reuse this research rather than creating a duplicate investigation; create a new research/task ticket only if a decision needs a specific missing fact or access step.
